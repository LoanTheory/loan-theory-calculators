More information

How HomeStart repayments are calculated: Your repayment is fixed at your original loan amount divided by a set factor (160 for a Low Deposit Loan, 170 for a Graduate or Shared Equity loan). Interest is charged each month on your current balance at your HomeStart rate. Because the repayment amount doesn't automatically adjust to cover the interest owed, your loan balance can increase over time rather than steadily reducing.

How the mainstream lender repayment is calculated: This uses a standard principal-and-interest amortisation schedule at the mainstream rate shown, over the loan term. Each repayment covers that month's interest plus a portion of the loan balance, so the balance steadily reduces to zero by the end of the term.

A full lender assessment is required before any refinancing recommendation can be made, and actual savings may differ from the figures shown based on your individual circumstances, the lender's assessment, fees, and final approved rate. All loan applications are subject to lender credit criteria, fees and charges.
